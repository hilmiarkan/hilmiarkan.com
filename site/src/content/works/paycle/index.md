## Project Overview

Paycle is one of my freelance UI Design projects on Upwork. I helped design a web app for transferring money abroad, focused on a student who wants to pay a college loan in the US

## The challenge

I had to translate my client request document into the design she desired. It was a tough job to do, seeing my client is a project manager, so not the actual client that needed the design. It was all a communication problem. But it was all ended well.
